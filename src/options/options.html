Semantic Highlighter

Press Ctrl+Shift+F or click the toolbar button, then type an idea in the search box and press Enter, or select a passage. Sentences close in meaning turn warm, unrelated ones cool. Esc clears; the same shortcut, pressed in the search box, turns it off. Shortcuts can be changed at chrome://extensions/shortcuts, or in Firefox under about:addons → ⚙ → Manage Extension Shortcuts.
